# Citations and Verification — Flagged Items

**Manuscript:** *Beyond Simandou: The Productive Republic — A Doctrine for the Guinean State*
**Author:** Abdourahamane Diallo
**Purpose of this document:** The brief asked that areas needing citations or verification be *flagged*, not changed. Accordingly, **no figure in the manuscript has been altered.** Every quantitative claim and every comparative factual claim is listed below with its location in the book, so the author can attach a source or verify the figure before commercial publication.

These fall into two groups: (A) **quantitative claims about Guinea** — the empirical backbone of the argument; these are the highest priority to source, and (B) **comparative / factual claims about other countries and migration routes** — generally accepted but worth a citation in a published nonfiction work.

## Recommended handling

1. **Before commercial publication**, attach sources for Group A (items 1–14). These are load-bearing: the book's central diagnosis rests on them.
2. For Group B, a short **endnotes section** or a "Sources and Further Reading" page is sufficient.
3. The manuscript already contains a back-matter page, **"A Note on Figures and Sources,"** which transparently tells readers the figures are the author's working evidence base and that citations will follow. This keeps the first edition honest without inventing references. Replace or expand it once sources are gathered.
4. **No numbers were changed or "corrected."** Where a percentage was written as a numeral mid-sentence in the source (e.g., a figure that began a new line), it was only reformatted for typographic consistency (see the editorial report); the value is identical.
